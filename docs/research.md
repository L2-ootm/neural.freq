# Evidence notes

Summaries come from abstracts, not full texts. Overall strength: mixed.

| Source | Finding | Use in app |
|---|---|---|
| Garcia-Argibay, Santed, Reales (2019), Psychological Research 83(2):357-372, doi:10.1007/s00426-018-1066-8 | Meta-analysis, 22 studies, pooled g = 0.45; effect depends on frequency, exposure and timing | About screen, labeled small-to-medium and non-specific |
| Ingendoh, Posny, Heine (2023), PLOS ONE, doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0286023 | Systematic review of 14 EEG studies: 5 support entrainment, 8 contradict, 1 mixed | About screen |
| Perrott and Nelson; Licklider (secondary summaries) | Beats heard as a beat up to about 20 Hz, carriers below about 1 kHz | Note that 40 Hz is outside the usual range |
| Kellet refined pink filter | Coefficients published for 44.1 kHz | Slope verified at 44.1 and 48 kHz in `tests/noise.test.ts` (-10 dB/decade within 1.5 dB) |
| WHO-ITU H.870 (secondary summary) | Weekly safe-listening dose framework | Rationale for the level ceiling; the app cannot measure sound pressure |

Not claimed anywhere: any benefit for sleep, focus, memory, mood or any condition.
